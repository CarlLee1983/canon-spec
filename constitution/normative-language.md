# Normative Language

## Normative

- `MUST` and `SHALL` identify an unconditional requirement.
- `MUST NOT` and `SHALL NOT` identify an unconditional prohibition.
- `MAY` identifies a permitted choice that cannot alter observable contract behavior.
- Lowercase descriptive prose is explanatory and MUST NOT create a requirement.
- A normative statement MUST have exactly one authoritative location. Other artifacts MUST cite its stable identifier.
- Examples MUST NOT override normative statements.

## Explanatory

Uppercase requirement words make machine review and human review easier. Canon Spec treats `MUST` and `SHALL` as equivalent in strength; authors should prefer `MUST` for consistency.
